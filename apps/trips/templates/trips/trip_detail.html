{% comment %} The comment threads are the one part of this page that is *not* part of the printed artifact. A PDF of the itinerary is what gets shared and archived; a working conversation between the people planning it is not. Every block is no-print so "Print → Save as PDF" leaves them off, which also means the `break-inside: avoid` rules never have to reason about them. They live here rather than only on the editing pages because those require `edit`: a commentor who is not an editor would have nowhere to read back what they wrote. See `_trip_comments` for why this is one query. {% endcomment %}
