First the Plan (budget-estimates B2; budget_plan.plan over the trip page's context): rows by category — each item's estimate on its basis, a costed booking once over what it covers, chosen options, the daily food allowance; undecided choices as a range outside the totals — with Planned / of which booked / Paid / Left to pay per category and overall, and each traveller's share against what they've paid, all in ?in= (the reader's currency by default).
