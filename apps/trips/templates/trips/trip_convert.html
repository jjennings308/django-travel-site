Turn decisions into choices

To-dos with options become “choose one” groups on their days, which everyone can vote on and an editor picks (a chosen option stays picked). When the day already has a choice that looks like the same one, the options join it. The to-do stays as the reminder to book, with a note saying where its options went. Nothing is lost. {{ count }} to convert.
