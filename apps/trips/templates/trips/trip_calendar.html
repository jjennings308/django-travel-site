Add to calendar {% include "partials/_help_link.html" with slug="calendar-feed" label="the calendar feed" %}

Subscribe once; your calendar app picks up changes to the trip by itself, every few hours.

Your calendar link

This link is yours alone. It shows the trip's journeys, stays, activities and booked meals with their confirmation numbers, to anyone who has it, without logging in. Don't share it; to let someone else see the trip, invite them on People.

How to subscribe

Google Calendar (on a computer)
Next to Other calendars, click + → From URL, paste the link, then Add calendar. It then shows on your phone too.
iPhone or iPad
Open in Calendar, or Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste the link.
Mac
In Calendar, File → New Calendar Subscription, paste the link.
Outlook
Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the link.

Flights and trains show at their real times wherever you are; activities and dinners show at the local time on the day.

Reset the link

If the link got out, reset it. The old link stops working at once; subscribe again with the new one.
