{% comment %} Renders a form's visible fields with per-field help, but skips fields a caller lists as hidden. The `not hide_fields or` matters: without it, a caller that passes no hide_fields renders *nothing* (`x not in ` is an error, which the template engine turns into False). Django has no built-in "render everything except these", and the trip form uses `as_p` while the day form needs a rendered hint under `travelers`. This partial exists so both get the same markup, and so a field added to a form appears without a template edit. A short item form (forms.MoreDetailsMixin, short-forms F1) names fields in MORE: they render in a closed "More details" fold after the others, its summary listing what's filled; it opens by itself when one of them has an error. Cost is / People / Rooms come there as partials/_cost_more.html. Forms without the mixin are unchanged. {% endcomment %}
