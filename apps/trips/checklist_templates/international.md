title: International trip
kind: documents
summary: Passports, entry rules, money and insurance for a trip abroad.

- [each] Passport valid for 6 months after you come home
- [each] Visa, ETIAS or ESTA if the country needs one
- [each] Photo or scan of your passport, kept separately
- [each] Travel insurance details (policy number and emergency phone)
- [each] Bank and credit cards that work abroad, bank told about the trip
- [each] Phone plan or eSIM for abroad
- Some local cash for the first day
- Plug adapters for the country
- Copies of bookings and tickets (or saved for offline on this site)
- Driving licence and International Driving Permit if renting a car
- Emergency contacts written down
