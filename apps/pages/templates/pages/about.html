About {{ SITE_NAME }}

A place to keep the things you want to do in the world, and to actually get round to doing them.

Why it exists

Most of us carry a list in our heads: see the northern lights, go to Oktoberfest, watch the Steelers play at home, walk the Arches. The list rarely turns into plans. The ideas are scattered across notes, bookmarks and conversations, the dates come and go, and the trip, if it happens, lives in a different set of emails and spreadsheets.

{{ SITE_NAME }} keeps all of it in one place, from the first idea to the photos afterwards, so a wish has a clear next step at every stage.

How it works

  1. Discover activities, events and places, or add your own.
  2. Dream: put what you like on your bucket list.
  3. Pick a date from the upcoming dates we know about.
  4. Plan the trip with the people going: itinerary, bookings, budget and photos.
  5. Done: tick it off with a rating, notes and photos, and keep it in My adventures.

See every feature or read how it works in more detail.

What we care about

  • Your list is yours. Bucket lists and trips are private unless you choose to share them. A shared trip link shows an outline, never bookings, confirmation numbers or who's travelling. See our privacy policy.
  • Honest information. The numbers on this site are real counts, and reviews come from members, marked “Been there” when the reviewer ticked it off.
  • Plans with other people. Trips are made to be shared with the people going, each with the access they need.
