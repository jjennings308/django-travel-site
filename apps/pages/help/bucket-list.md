# Your bucket list

Your bucket list follows each goal from an idea to a trip you've taken: **Dream**, **Pick a date**, **Plan**, **Done**. The left menu is grouped the same way.

## Add a goal

- From the catalogue: on any activity, event or place, click **Add to bucket list**.
- Your own: in **My bucket list**, click **Add a goal**, type **What do you want to do?** and click **Add to bucket list**. While you type, the site suggests matching activities, events and places ("Is it one of these?"); picking one fills in the place and dates when you plan a trip.

You can give a goal a priority, categories, a budget, who it's with and what inspired it. A goal you added your own way can be linked to the catalogue later with **Link to the catalogue** on its edit page.

## Give it a date

**My dates** lists your goals by when they happen:

- **Coming up**: goals with a date, each with **Plan a trip**.
- **Needs a date**: for an activity, the site offers its next dates (**Upcoming:**); or click **Set dates** to choose your own.
- **Date has passed: did you do it?**: click **I did it**, or give it a **New date**.

## Plan a trip

On a goal, click **Plan a trip**. Check the trip's name, place, **First day** and **Last day**, and click **Start the trip**. You become the trip's editor ([starting a trip](/help/starting-a-trip/)).

Several goals can share a trip: under **Or add it to a trip you already have**, click **Add to this trip**. On the trip, a link lists your goals on it.

## Tick it off

Click **Done** on a goal. Give it **Your rating**, write **How was it?**, add up to 10 **Photos (optional)**, and click **Mark as done**. For a goal from the catalogue, tick **Share as a public review** to post your rating and notes as a review, marked **Been there**.

A trip that's over shows **Trip over: tick it off** on its goal, and a prompt on the trip page.

**My adventures** keeps everything you've done, by year, with its photos.

## Photos and albums

A goal can have photos at any stage: click its **Photos** link. **My albums** gathers photos into albums: click **New album**, then add photos from your goals and trips (**Add the ticked photos**) or upload new ones. **Make cover** picks the album's cover; **Take out** removes a photo from the album without deleting it.

Photos count towards your 1 GB of storage for photos and tickets; see [storage](/help/reporting-and-safety/#storage).

## Badges

Badges mark milestones: your first goal (**Dreamer**), ten goals (**Big dreamer**), goals ticked off (**First adventure**, **Adventurer**, **Seasoned traveller**, **Legend**), goals done in three or ten countries (**Globetrotter**, **World explorer**), your first event (**Front row**), your first trip for a goal (**Trip planner**) and your first photo (**Snapshot**). They show on your dashboard and in **My adventures**.

## Who can see it

Your bucket list is private. A goal marked **Show on my public bucket list** appears on your public page, but only when your profile is set to public. Albums follow the same rule. Your public page can be opened by anyone with its link, but it asks search engines not to list it.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No dates offered under **Needs a date** | The activity has no upcoming dates listed. Set your own, or add one ([places and events](/help/places-and-events/)). |
| My public page is empty | Set your profile to public, and mark the goals you want to show. |
| A goal is on the wrong trip | On the trip's goals page, remove it, then add it to the right trip. |
