# Reading your trip

Everything about a trip is on one page, built to read on a phone as well as a desk. Open **Trips** in the left menu and click a trip.

## The top of the page

Under the trip's name you see its dates, how many nights, and its planning stage (for example **Confirming**, or **Completed** once it's over).

| Button or link | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit trip** | Change the trip. Only editors see it ([who can do what](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what)). |
| **Download PDF** | The printable booklet ([see below](#booklet)). |
| **Map** · **Budget** · **Photos** | The trip's map, its shared costs, and the shared photo album. |
| **⋯** | **Add to calendar** ([calendar feed](/help/calendar-feed/)), and sharing and deleting when you're allowed to ([sharing](/help/sharing-and-access/#the-public-share-link)). Editors also find **Tidy suggestions** here ([tidying an itinerary](/help/tidying-an-itinerary/)). |

When something needs attention, a **Needs attention** banner sits under the title. It counts what's **not booked**, what's still **to do**, open **decisions**, **documents not ticked** on a checklist, and journeys **not on any day**. Each one links to its list.

The bar under the title jumps to the two parts of the page, **Days** and **Reference**. It stays at the top as you scroll.

## Days at a glance

The folded days are the trip at a glance. Each day's first line has its number, date, city and theme. The second line has what's set for the day:

| Part | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| 🛏 | Where you sleep that night, with **(check in)** on the night you arrive, or **(check out)** on a last morning with nowhere booked that night. It links to the stay's card. |
| **✈ overnight** | A flight that arrives the next day. |
| Travel | The day's journeys, such as "🚆 Frankfurt → Augsburg", or "🚆 2 legs · ~3 h 15 min". |
| Highlights | Up to two of the day's activities. |
| ⚠ | How many things on that day are not booked yet or still to do. It links to the banner. |

A day shows only the parts it has. Click a day to open it.

Above the days:

- **About this trip**, folded: **Travelling**, **Cities** and **Destination**, your goals for the trip, the trip's notes, and the **Estimated trip cost** when bookings or activities have costs: their total in your currency ([the budget](/help/budget/#estimated-trip-cost)). Once the trip is over it also has **Rate the places from this trip** ([rating a finished trip](/help/places-and-events/#rating-a-finished-trip)).
- **Journeys not on any day** and **Stays outside the trip's days**, when there are any: bookings whose dates fall outside the trip's days, shown in full so nothing is hidden.

## Days

Each day is folded to its summary ([see above](#days-at-a-glance)). Click a day to open it. The page opens today's day while the trip is on, Day 1 before it starts, and none once it's over. **Expand all** and **Collapse all** open or fold every day.

An open day reads from top to bottom:

1. **Where you sleep.** On the day you arrive at a hotel you see its full card: address, nights, rate, confirmation number and notes. On the other nights you see **Sleeping:** with the hotel's name, and on the last morning **Check out:**. Both link to the card.
2. **The timeline.** Trains, flights, activities and booked meals, in time order. Each has its time, what it is, its confirmation number and, if it isn't booked yet, a **Still to book** line. Times of trains and flights are always local to where they leave and arrive, wherever you are reading from. **Travel:** above the timeline adds up the day's journeys.
3. **Any time.** Things without a set time: planned journeys in their order, then activities.
4. **The write-up.** The day's plans, tips, tables and choices, in the order they were written.
5. **Good to know.** Phone numbers and tips for the whole day.
6. **Food ideas.** Restaurants without a booked time.

Contacts and tips linked to a hotel, train or activity appear right under it: a **☎** line with a phone number you can tap to call, or an **ⓘ** line you tap to read.

Editors also see **Edit this day** at the top of each open day, and **Edit** beside each journey, activity and meal.

## Reference

The home of the trip's confirmations, contacts and to-dos. Everything elsewhere on the page links here.

- **Confirmations**, grouped as Flights, Trains, Stays, Activities, Meals and Other. Each shows the number in large type (easy to read out at a desk), who it's with, what it covers (click to jump to it), who it's for, and **Manage booking ↗** when there's a link. Insurance and passes are under Other.
- **Still to book**: tasks, most urgent first, each saying what it's for and on which day. A decision reads **Decide:** with its options, the chosen one ticked. **All to-dos** opens the trip's [To do page](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/#the-to-do-page), where editors add and tick them off.
- **Not booked yet**: flights, trains, stays and bookable activities that have no confirmation and no task yet.
- **Contacts**: name, role, phone (tap to call), email, website and address, with **On:** links to the days they matter.
- **Info**: the trip's know-how, such as ticket rules or a tent guide, with **On:** links.

A confirmation number on a hotel, train or activity is a small chip; click it to jump to its full entry here. **📎 2** on a chip means it has two tickets or documents; they're listed on its row in Reference. Click one to open it ([tickets and documents](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/#tickets-and-documents)).

## Booklet

**Download PDF** gives a booklet to print or keep offline. It has:

- a cover with the dates and who's travelling;
- **Days at a glance**, one row per day;
- a section per day: where you stay, **Getting around**, the plans, **Activities**, **Food** and **Good to know**, with confirmation numbers and phone numbers printed in full on the day they're needed;
- **Reference**: **Confirmations**, **Where we're staying**, **Contacts**, **Info** and **Still to book**.

Paper can't link, so the booklet repeats contacts and confirmation numbers on each day they matter, as well as in Reference.

## Offline

On a phone, the trip page keeps working without a signal (on a train, or abroad without data):

- Each time you open a trip page, your phone keeps a copy of it.
- To be sure before you go, open **⋯** and click **Save for offline**. That also keeps the PDF booklet and the trip's tickets and documents (see [tickets and documents](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/#tickets-and-documents)).

With no signal, the trip page opens from that copy, with **Offline — this is the copy saved …** at the top. Anything changed since then isn't shown, and you can't make changes until you're back online. Edit pages, the map and other pages need a signal.

The copy is only for you and only on that device. Signing out deletes it, so a shared phone or computer keeps nothing.

To put the site on your home screen, use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone: the share button; Android: the ⋮ menu).

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A day won't open from a link | Click the day's line to open it, or **Expand all**. |
| A train shows on the wrong day | A journey appears on the day it leaves. Ask an editor to check its date. |
| No **Edit trip** button | You can read the trip but not change it. See [sharing and access](/help/sharing-and-access/). |
| The trip page doesn't open without a signal | Open it once with a signal (or use **Save for offline**) on that device first. Private or incognito windows don't keep copies. |
| **Download PDF** says the booklet can't be made yet | Use your browser's **Print** → **Save as PDF** for now, and tell us. |
