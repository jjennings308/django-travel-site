# Choices, options and votes

Not everything is decided when you plan. A **choice** (or "choose one") sits on a day with two or more **options**: dinner at Augustiner or the Hofbräuhaus, the castle or the museum for the afternoon, which hotel in Munich. Everyone on the trip can see the options and vote. When you've decided, an editor picks one, or splits the choice so different people take different options.

## Reading a choice

An undecided choice is a box on its day, marked **Undecided**. If it has a time it sits in the day's timeline at that time; otherwise it comes after the timeline. Each option shows:

- its title and notes, and a **More ↗** link if it has one;
- what's in it: journeys, a stay, activities, meals or notes, shown as they would be on the day;
- its estimated cost (the option's own estimate plus its activities' and journeys' costs; in their currency, or the trip's home currency when they mix) and how long it takes, when that's known;
- **👍** and how many people voted for it, with their names;
- when it's linked to a catalogue place, activity or event, **ⓘ N notes from the catalogue** ("Closed Mondays", "Book 3 months ahead"), folded: click to read them before you pick ([Good to know notes](/help/places-and-events/#good-to-know-notes)).

On the trip's **Map**, the **Choices** switch (off at first) shows the options of undecided choices that have a place as faint pins, with their votes.

An undecided choice adds one to the day's **⚠** and to **Needs attention** ("N decisions", which links to the first one). The [To do page](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/#the-to-do-page) lists every undecided choice at the top.

**Until an option is picked, nothing in it counts.** Its trains, stays, meals and activities aren't in the day's timeline, in **Not booked yet**, in the trip's cost estimate, on the map or in the [calendar feed](/help/calendar-feed/). The calendar shows one all-day event instead, "Undecided: Castle / Deutsches Museum". The shared read-only link never shows choices.

## Voting

Anyone who can comment on the trip can vote: click **👍** on the option you'd like. Click it again to take your vote back. You have one vote per option, so you can vote for more than one. Viewers see the votes but can't vote.

## Picking

Editors click **Pick** on an option. Its items then count as the day's own: they join the timeline, Not booked yet, the cost estimate, the map and the calendar. The other options aren't deleted: the day shows **Chosen from: Afternoon (2 options) — Castle**, which opens the options **Considered**, with their votes. **Reopen the choice** there makes it undecided again. **Save for someday** beside a considered option adds it to your own bucket list ([ideas and goals](/help/ideas-and-goals/#save-for-someday)).

### Picking a stay, meal, activity or journey adds it to the day

On a choice that says what it's about ([below](#what-a-choice-is-about)), **Pick** turns the option into the real thing on the day, and the trip page opens it beside the days so you can add the address, times and confirmation:

| Kind | Picking adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Stay** | a stay from the choice's day for the option's nights (its own, else the choice's; one if neither says), with its city, address or place, nightly price and notes |
| **Eat** | a meal on the day at the choice's time, at the option's catalogue place or typed name |
| **Do** | an activity on the day from the choice's time until its end, at its catalogue place, activity's venue or event (an event becomes a ticket) |
| **Travel** | a planned journey on the day by the choice's mode, from and to the option's places (matched to the trip's cities) |

The option's details are copied once: from then on the stay, meal, activity or journey is the record, and changing the option doesn't change it. The new item sits inside its option, so it counts while the option is picked: **Reopen the choice** keeps it, under the option in **Considered**, and picking that option again uses it again instead of making another. Picking another option makes that option's item. An option you already put something in by hand (**In option**) makes nothing new. **Split** makes the item for each option someone takes, on their track.

Picking and reopening are in the trip's [history](/help/trip-history/), and so is the item a pick adds; **Undo** on a pick reopens the choice (the item stays, under its option).

## Splitting a choice

Sometimes you go different ways: two of you take the castle, the others the museum. Editors click **Split** on an undecided choice (on the day, or **Split between people** on the choice's page). Its page has a row for each person on the day (the day's own group if it has one, else everyone on the trip): choose an option for each person and click **Split**. Everyone has to be placed, and at least two options need someone; if everyone goes the same way, **Pick** that option instead.

Every option someone takes then counts, each as a **track** on the day: a box marked **Split** with a column per track (stacked on a phone), headed by who's on it ("Debbie, John"), with that track's own timeline, notes, stay and cost. Things on the day that aren't in an option stay above and below it, for everyone. Options nobody takes fold into **Considered**.

- **Not booked yet**, the cost estimate and the map include every track, since all of them happen.
- In the [calendar feed](/help/calendar-feed/), **Only the things I'm on** follows your track: you see your track's activities, journeys, meals and stay, not the other track's. An activity's own travellers still win.
- Journeys and stays in an option follow it: "we take the 17:05 back, they stay for dinner and take the 21:30" is two options, each with its own train. A stay in a track shows its other nights as "Sleeping (Debbie, John): …".

**Change who goes** on the split box changes the tracks; **Reopen the choice** makes it undecided again. Splitting is in the trip's [history](/help/trip-history/), and **Undo** on it reopens the choice.

## Making a choice

Editors make a choice on a day in either of two ways:

- **+ Add** at the end of the day: type what's being decided ("Afternoon") and click **Options** ([adding a day](/help/adding-a-day/#add-to-a-day)).
- The day's page: **+ Choose one** under **Add to this day**.
- An idea on the trip's Ideas shelf: **Add as an option** ([ideas and goals](/help/ideas-and-goals/#making-an-idea-an-option)).

Either opens the choice's page: what's being decided, **What is this choice about?** ([below](#what-a-choice-is-about)), an optional time (it then sits in the timeline), notes, and its **Options** (title, notes, a link, an estimated cost, and what the kind asks for). Save, and each saved option gets a box to add things straight into it: type a line such as "Train Munich → Füssen 9:15" or "Dinner Augustiner 7pm", as in + Add.

To put something already on the day into an option, open it (its panel or page) and choose the option under **In option**. Set it back to blank to take it out. Deleting a choice keeps everything that was in its options, simply on the day.

## What a choice is about

A choice can say what kind of thing it decides, so each option asks only for what you need to compare them:

| Kind | The choice has | Each option has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stay** | how many **nights**, from its day | a catalogue city or place, or an address; a **cost per night**; its own nights if it differs (a 3-night minimum) |
| **Eat** | the **meal** and its time | a catalogue place (Food & drink first) or a typed one; a cost |
| **Do** | a **time** and **until** | a catalogue place, activity or event, or a typed place; a cost |
| **Travel** | the **mode** (train, flight…) and a time | **from** and **to**; a cost |
| **Anything** | a time | just a title, notes, a link and a cost, as before |

The site guesses the kind from what you type: "Where to stay in Munich" or "Augsburg hotel" is a stay, "Dinner Friday" eating out (dinner), "Train or drive to Füssen" travel, anything else something to do. Change it on the choice's page and save: the options then ask for that kind's details. Choices made before kinds existed are **Anything** and work as before.

On the day, each option shows a line for its kind: a stay "EUR 95.00/night × 3 = EUR 285.00 · Maximilianstraße", a meal "Augustiner Bräustuben · EUR 40.00", something to do "Hacker-Festzelt · 11:00–16:00", a journey "Train · Augsburg → Munich". A stay's cost counts as nightly price × nights, in the option's total and the trip's estimated cost. The PDF prints the same lines.

## From older trips

Trips planned before choices had **Choose your adventure** sections and **decision** to-dos (to-dos with options) instead. Every Choose your adventure has been turned into a choice on its day (one with a confirmation number on it became a note listing its options, so the confirmation stays with it), and a Word itinerary imported now makes choices straight away. Choices live on the day; to-dos are only for booking, and they no longer take options.

A to-do that still has options shows **Make it a choice** to editors (in Reference, on the To do page and on the to-do's own page), and **Needs attention** shows **N decisions to turn into choices**, which opens the same page for all of them. Each row says where its options go:

- **Merge into “Where to stay in Augsburg” (Day 4)** when its day already has an undecided choice that looks like the same one (a word in common, such as "Augsburg", or an option with the same name). Only the options the choice doesn't have yet are added; if the to-do had chosen one, it's picked.
- **New choice on Day 4** otherwise: a choice with the to-do's title as its question, its options, and its chosen one picked.

Use the select on a row to change it, for instance to make a new choice instead of merging. When two choices on the day look like it, the row asks you to pick one. The to-do goes on its own day, else its journey's, stay's or activity's day; one with no day at all makes a new choice on Day 1 (flagged to check) and never merges.

The to-do itself stays on the list as the reminder to book, with its priority and due date, and a note saying where its options went ("Options moved to the choice “…” on Day 4"). Tick **Tick it off instead** on a row to mark it done instead.

## In the PDF

An undecided choice prints as "Afternoon — undecided" with each option, its line for the choice's kind, and what's in it. A picked one prints what was chosen, with its items among the day's own. A split one prints its tracks with who's on each: side by side when there are two, one under another when there are more.
