# Starting a trip

There are two ways to start a trip. Either way you become its editor.

## From a goal on your bucket list

The quickest way. On a goal in **My bucket list** or **My dates**, click **Plan a trip**.

1. The trip's name, dates and place are filled in from the goal; change them if you like. "Trip dates can change later."
2. Click **Start the trip**.

To put the goal on a trip you already have instead, pick it under **Or add it to a trip you already have** and click **Add to this trip**. One trip can cover several goals, each an idea on the trip's Ideas shelf ([ideas and goals](/help/ideas-and-goals/)). See [your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/#plan-a-trip).

## From scratch

Open **Trips** in the left menu and click **New trip** (or **Start a trip** when you have none yet).

Fill in **The trip** and click **Create trip**:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Name | What you call the trip, such as "James & Regan — Taos". Only people on the trip see it. |
| Destination | Where it is, such as "Taos / Angel Fire, New Mexico". A shared link uses this, never the name. |
| Cities | The catalogue cities the trip visits, as chips: type to add one, × to remove it. You rarely need to: every city a day, stay or journey uses is added by itself, and a city something uses can't be removed ("Munich is used by Day 3, Hotel Laimer Hof"). |
| Start date, End date | The first and last day. |
| Travelers | Who's going. Use **Add a traveler** below if a name is missing. |
| Budget, Structure, Status | The budget level, the kind of trip, and how far planning has got (Starting → Fleshing out → Confirming → Ready to go). |
| Notes | Anything for the whole trip. |

## The route

The quickest way to lay out a trip: on the trip page click **Set the route** (or **Edit route** next to the route at the top of the page, or **Set the route** under Days on the trip's Settings). Give each stop a city and its number of nights, in order: Munich 3, Salzburg 2, Vienna 4. Type part of a city's name to find it; the trip's own cities are offered before you type.

Click **Preview** to see what saving would do, then **Save the route**:

- Each day gets its city. The day you travel belongs to the next city, and the last city also has the day you leave. Days the trip doesn't have yet are made.
- A day that already has a different city (a day trip) is kept, unless you tick **Replace days that already have a different city**.
- Ticked by default: a **planned leg** on each travel day ("Munich → Salzburg", no times yet; skipped where that day already has one), and a **stay to book** in each city ("Stay in Munich"; skipped where a stay already covers those nights). Both show under **To book** until you fill them in.
- When the nights don't add up to the trip, the preview says so. A longer route stops at the trip's last day unless you tick **Extend the trip to fit the route**; a shorter one leaves the last days without a city.

Need more stops? **Preview** adds three more rows. Clear a stop's city to drop it. Saving is one line in the trip's [history](/help/trip-history/).

The route then shows at the top of the trip page as "Munich (3) → Salzburg (2) → Vienna (4)", each city linking to its first day.

## Finding a city

Every city box on the trip's pages (a day's city, a stay's, a journey's From and To, the route) works the same way: type part of the name and pick it from the list. Accents and small typos don't matter ("munchen" finds München). Before you type, the trip's own cities are offered as buttons.

A city that isn't in the catalogue yet: type its name and click **Add “Hallstatt” as a new city**, then pick its country (and region, if you like). You can use it straight away; others see it once staff have reviewed it. Without JavaScript the box is a plain list, with a **City not listed? Add it** link.

## What to do next

1. **The days**: set the route ([above](#the-route)), or add a day for each date on **Settings** ([adding a day](/help/adding-a-day/)).
2. On the trip page, use **+ Add** at the end of each open day: a stay for where you sleep ([stays](/help/adding-a-day/#stays)), trains, flights and drives ([travel legs](/help/travel-legs/)), activities, meals and notes. Click anything on a day to open it beside the page and change it.
3. Add confirmation numbers on the thing they confirm, once it's booked ([bookings](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/)).

**Settings** (at the top of the trip page, for editors) holds what belongs to the whole trip: its name, dates, status, cities, travellers, notes, the booklet's [cover photo](#a-cover-photo) and **home currency** (the one you think in, offered first wherever a cost is entered; [currency choices](/help/budget/#currency-choices)), the days table, **Loose confirmations** (bookings that cover no single item, such as travel insurance), **Contacts & info** and **Checklists**. Every stay and journey in date order is on **Bookings** ([the Bookings page](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/#the-bookings-page)).

## A cover photo

On **Settings**, under **Booklet cover**, choose a photo for the front of the PDF booklet ([the booklet](/help/reading-your-trip/#booklet)), then click **Save changes**:

- **Upload a cover photo**: pick a picture from your device; a preview shows before you save. It also joins the trip's **Photos**.
- **Or choose one of the trip's photos**: click one of the photos already in the trip's album. The one on the cover is ticked.
- **Take the photo off the cover** leaves the cover plain; the photo stays in the trip's photos.

On **Photos**, **Use on the booklet** under a photo does the same; the one in use is marked "On the booklet". A wide picture fills the cover best: it's cropped to the top half of the page. Only editors can choose it, and you can also add one when you create the trip.

## Changing the dates

Change **Start date** or **End date** on **Settings** and save. If the days no longer fit the new dates, the next page lists what to do: **Add a day for** each new date without one (ticked), and **Remove** each day now outside the dates (ticked only when nothing is on it; otherwise it says what would be deleted, such as "2 meals, 1 note"). Journeys and stays aren't deleted with a day. Click **Fit the days**: the days are then numbered again in date order. **Leave them as they are** changes nothing; a day you keep shows under its date, outside the trip's dates.

To let others see or edit the trip, click **People** on the trip page and invite them ([inviting people](/help/sharing-and-access/#inviting-people)).

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **New trip** button | Your account can't start trips: it isn't verified yet (use the link in your sign-up email), or staff have stopped it. [Contact us](/contact/) if you think that's wrong. |
| "The end date cannot be before the start date." | Swap the dates. |
| A city isn't found | It isn't in the catalogue yet: add it from the city box ([finding a city](#finding-a-city)). |
| A city won't come off the trip's **Cities** | Something still uses it (the message names what). Change those first. |
| A day has no city after adding a stay | The stay needs a city, and the day only gets one if it had none. |
