# Starting a trip

There are two ways to start a trip. Either way you become its editor.

## From a goal on your bucket list

The easiest way, open to every member. On a goal in **My bucket list** or **My dates**, click **Plan a trip**.

1. The trip's name, dates and place are filled in from the goal; change them if you like. "Trip dates can change later."
2. Click **Start the trip**.

To put the goal on a trip you already have instead, pick it under **Or add it to a trip you already have** and click **Add to this trip**. One trip can cover several goals. See [your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/#plan-a-trip).

## From scratch

Open **Trips** in the left menu and click **New trip**. You only see **New trip** if the site's staff have let you create trips; ask them if it's missing.

Fill in **The trip** and click **Create trip**:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Name | What you call the trip, such as "James & Regan — Taos". Only people on the trip see it. |
| Destination | Where it is, such as "Taos / Angel Fire, New Mexico". A shared link uses this, never the name. |
| Cities | The catalogue cities the trip visits. Days and journeys pick their city from this list. |
| Start date, End date | The first and last day. |
| Travelers | Who's going. Use **Add a traveler** below if a name is missing. |
| Budget, Structure, Status | The budget level, the kind of trip, and how far planning has got (Starting → Fleshing out → Confirming → Ready to go). |
| Notes | Anything for the whole trip. |

## What to do next

On the trip page, click **Edit trip**. From there:

1. **Days**: click **Add a day** for each date ([adding a day](/help/adding-a-day/)).
2. **Lodging**: a row per stay, with its name, address, city, check-in and check-out. A stay's city fills in the city of the days it covers that have none.
3. **Travel by day**: the trains and flights for each day ([travel legs](/help/travel-legs/)).
4. **Bookings & confirmations**: confirmation numbers, once things are booked ([bookings](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/)).

Everything under **Trip details** saves together when you click **Save changes**; if one row has a mistake, nothing is written and the mistake is shown.

To let others see or edit the trip, see [sharing and access](/help/sharing-and-access/#getting-access).

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **New trip** button | Use **Plan a trip** on a bucket-list goal, or ask the site's staff to let you create trips. |
| "The end date cannot be before the start date." | Swap the dates. |
| A city isn't in the **Cities** list | It isn't in the catalogue yet. Add it under **Places**, or ask the site's staff. |
| A day has no city after adding a stay | The stay needs a city, and the day only gets one if it had none. |
