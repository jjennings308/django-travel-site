# How to add a day to your trip

A guide for trip editors.

## Before you start

You need **editor** access to the trip. If you can see a trip but have no **Settings** button at the top of its page, ask the trip's owner to make you an editor.

A day is one date of the trip. Everything that happens on that date hangs off it: train and flight legs, activities, meals, notes and tips. You add the day first, then fill it in.

Have these ready:

- The **date** (required).
- The **day number** (the site suggests the next one).
- Optionally: the **city** you spend the day in, a **one-line theme** such as "Oktoberfest — Munich day trip", and who is on the day if the group splits up.

## Add the day

It takes five steps; the day is saved at step 5.

1. Open **Trips** in the left menu and click your trip.
2. Click **Settings** at the top of the trip page.
3. Scroll down to the **Days** section, below the trip's main form.
4. Click **Add a day**. The "Add a day to …" page opens.
5. Fill in the fields (next section) and click **Add day**.

You land back on Settings with a green "Added day N" message, and the new day appears in the Days table.

## Fill in the day's details

Only the date and day number are required; leave the rest blank if you don't know yet.

| Field | What to enter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day number | Pre-filled with the next free number. Two days on one trip can't share a number. | 5 |
| Date | The calendar date of this day. Required. | Sep 21, 2027 |
| City | Where you *spend* the day, not where you sleep. Type to find it; the trip's cities are offered first. | Munich (even if you sleep in Augsburg) |
| Theme | A one-line summary shown as the day's title. | Oktoberfest — Munich day trip |
| Meals included | Tick only if meals are already covered by the trip price, such as on a tour. | Unticked |
| Travelers | Leave blank when the whole group is together. Pick names only on days the group splits. | Debbie, James, John, Regan |

The **City** box finds any city in the catalogue as you type, with the trip's own cities offered before you type ([finding a city](/help/starting-a-trip/#finding-a-city)); a city that isn't listed can be added from it. Whatever you pick joins the trip's cities. The same goes for a stay's city and a journey's From and To cities. A day covered by a stay picks up the stay's city automatically when the city is left blank. To set many days' cities at once, use [the route](/help/starting-a-trip/#the-route).

## Add to a day

The quickest way: open the day on the trip page and type one line in **+ Add to Day N** at the end of the day, then press Enter. The site works out what it is from the words and makes a rough version on that day, then opens it in the panel beside the page so you can fill in the rest ([open an item](/help/reading-your-trip/#open-an-item)):

| You type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| Train Munich → Salzburg 9:15 | **Travel**: a planned train from Munich to Salzburg on that day, "Around 09:15" in its notes. "Fly PIT -> ATL ~2h", "Drive Taos to Angel Fire ~45 min" and "Train to Salzburg" (from the day's city) work too. |
| Hotel Laimer Hof 3 nights | **Stay**: checking in that day, out three days later, in the day's city. Also "Stay at …", "Hostel …", "Airbnb …". |
| Dinner Augustiner 7:30pm | **Eat**: dinner at Augustiner with a 7:30 PM table, so it's on the timeline. Breakfast, brunch, lunch, coffee, drinks work the same; without a time it's a food idea. If the name matches a restaurant in the catalogue, its page offers to link it. |
| Tour of the salt mine 10am | **Do**: an activity at 10 AM. "Tickets …", "Museum …", "Visit …", or anything with a time. |
| Bring euros for the tents | **Note**: anything else becomes a note on the day. |

The words don't need to be exact, and nothing is guessed that you didn't type. If it guesses wrong, use a button instead of Enter: **Travel**, **Stay**, **Do**, **Eat** or **Note** makes that kind whatever the words say. **Options** starts a choice ("Afternoon") to put options in and vote on ([choices, options and votes](/help/options-and-votes/)). Without JavaScript, the new item opens on its own page.

## Notes

A **note** is the day's text: an optional heading, the text (one paragraph per line; start a line with • for a bullet) and a **tone**. None reads as plain text; **Tip**, **Warning** and **Critical** stand out in colour on the page and in the PDF.

Trips made before notes had phases, callouts and free text instead; every one has been turned into a note (a phase's heading, summary and highlights as • lines, a callout's text and tone, free text's paragraphs). A Word itinerary imported now becomes notes straight away.

## Add from the day page

A new day can also be filled from its **day page**. To get there, either click the day's **Edit** link in the Days table on Settings, or open the day on the trip page and click **Edit this day**.

At the top of the day page, **Add to this day** has a button for each kind of thing:

| Button | Use it for | What you enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| + Leg | A train, flight, drive or walk | From, To, Mode, a rough time ("45 min") and a note. Add exact times later, once booked. |
| + Stay | A hotel, flat or campsite you check in to that day | Name, address, city, check-in and check-out, rate, notes ([more below](#stays)). |
| + Activity | A tour, timed entry, ticket, table booking or car hire | Kind, title, provider, start and end time, place, cost, notes. |
| + Meal | A restaurant or food stop | Name, meal type, cuisine, price range, why it's recommended. Give it a **Reservation time** only if a table is booked. |
| + Contact or note | A phone number or tip for the whole day | A contact (name, phone, email) or an info note (title and text). |
| Section buttons | The day's write-up | **Note** (a heading, a tone and text: a part of the day, a tip or a warning; [notes](#notes)), **Travel notes** (a heading for the day's journeys, with an intro and rows for things like ticket prices). Options to pick from are a choice: **+ Choose one** ([choices](/help/options-and-votes/#making-a-choice)). |

Everything you add appears under **On this day**, in the same order as on the trip page. Each item has its own **Confirmations** and **Contacts & info** links for booking numbers and phone numbers.

Items with a time (legs, activities, booked meals) go on the day's timeline in time order. Meals without a reservation time appear under **Food ideas**.

## Stays

Add a stay from the day you check in: **+ Stay** opens a new stay with check-in on that day and check-out the next morning. Change the check-out date for a longer stay; one stay covers all its nights.

- **Address** finds the stay's pin on the trip map. Without an address the map uses the city's centre. To move the pin by hand, open **Adjust the pin**.
- **City**: the days the stay covers that have no city yet get this one (change a day trip by hand on its day).
- After **Add stay** you stay on the stay's page: add its confirmation number in **Confirmations** and the front desk's phone in **Contacts & info** there ([bookings and confirmations](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/)).

The stay then shows on every day it covers under **On this day**: **Check in** on the first day, **Sleeping** on the nights in between and **Check out** on the last morning, each with **Edit**. On the trip page, editors find **Edit** on the stay's card.

## Link a meal or section to the catalogue

A meal can name its catalogue **Place** (in the Meals form), a section a **Place**, an **Activity** or an **Event** (on the section's page), and an activity an **Event** (a dated one during the trip, such as Oktoberfest 2027). A section named like an activity on a day its event runs is offered the event ("Looks like the catalogue event Oktoberfest 2027"). The lists offer only places and activities in the trip's cities, so add the city to the trip first. It's optional: most meals stay plain text, and only notable restaurants are in the catalogue. A linked item shows the catalogue name as a link on the trip page and as plain text in the booklet. Linking also brings in the place's or activity's **Good to know** notes, on the trip page and in the PDF ([notes](/help/places-and-events/#good-to-know-notes)).

When a meal's name or a section's title is the name of one catalogue place or activity in the trip's cities (capitals, punctuation and accents don't matter, and German ö, ä, ü may be written oe, ae, ue), **On this day** shows **Looks like … — Link / Not this one**. Nothing is linked until you click **Link**; **Not this one** stops suggesting it.

## Check it on the trip page and in the booklet

Open the trip page. Your day appears in two places:

- **Days:** the day itself, folded to its summary: its date, city and theme, then where you sleep, travel and highlights. Click it to open it and check the timeline, write-up and food ideas. To fix something there, click it: it opens in a panel beside the page, where you can change and save it without leaving the trip ([open an item](/help/reading-your-trip/#open-an-item)). A meal has its own page too now, with its confirmations and contacts.
- **Download PDF:** the printed booklet gives the day its own section, with its stay, legs, activities and meals.

## Tips and troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Day N already exists on this trip." | Pick an unused day number. Each day of a trip needs its own number. |
| No **Settings** button | You have view-only access. Ask the trip's owner to make you an editor. |
| The city you want isn't found | It isn't in the catalogue yet: click **Add “…” as a new city** in the box, or leave the city blank. |
| A train or flight shows on the wrong day | Edit the leg and correct its date. A leg appears on the day whose date matches its departure. |
| Need to change a day | Settings → Days → **Edit**, change the fields, then click **Save day**. |
| Need to remove a day | On **Settings**, in the Days table, click the day's **Delete** link and confirm. This permanently removes the day with its meals and sections; booking tasks that pointed at it are kept. |

Tip: add a **Note** with a heading for each part of the day first (Morning, Afternoon, Evening). Then add the legs, activities and meals with times, so the timeline and the write-up line up.
