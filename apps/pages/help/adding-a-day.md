# How to add a day to your trip

A guide for trip editors.

## Before you start

You need **editor** access to the trip. If you can see a trip but have no **Edit trip** button at the top of its page, ask the trip's owner to make you an editor.

A day is one date of the trip. Everything that happens on that date hangs off it: train and flight legs, activities, meals, notes and tips. You add the day first, then fill it in.

Have these ready:

- The **date** (required).
- The **day number** (the site suggests the next one).
- Optionally: the **city** you spend the day in, a **one-line theme** such as "Oktoberfest — Munich day trip", and who is on the day if the group splits up.

## Add the day

It takes five steps; the day is saved at step 5.

1. Open **Trips** in the left menu and click your trip.
2. Click **Edit trip** at the top of the trip page.
3. Scroll down to the **Days** section, below the trip's main form.
4. Click **Add a day**. The "Add a day to …" page opens.
5. Fill in the fields (next section) and click **Add day**.

You land back on Edit trip with a green "Added day N" message, and the new day appears in the Days table.

## Fill in the day's details

Only the date and day number are required; leave the rest blank if you don't know yet.

| Field | What to enter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day number | Pre-filled with the next free number. Two days on one trip can't share a number. | 5 |
| Date | The calendar date of this day. Required. | Sep 21, 2027 |
| City | Where you *spend* the day, not where you sleep. The list shows the trip's cities. | Munich (even if you sleep in Augsburg) |
| Theme | A one-line summary shown as the day's title. | Oktoberfest — Munich day trip |
| Meals included | Tick only if meals are already covered by the trip price, such as on a tour. | Unticked |
| Travelers | Leave blank when the whole group is together. Pick names only on days the group splits. | Debbie, James, John, Regan |

If the city you need isn't in the list, add it to the trip first under **Edit trip**. A day covered by a stay picks up the stay's city automatically when the city is left blank.

## Add what happens that day

A new day starts empty; you fill it from its **day page**. To get there, either click the day's **Edit** link in the Days table on Edit trip, or open the day on the trip page and click **Edit this day**.

At the top of the day page, **Add to this day** has a button for each kind of thing:

| Button | Use it for | What you enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| + Leg | A train, flight, drive or walk | From, To, Mode, a rough time ("45 min") and a note. Add exact times later, once booked. |
| + Activity | A tour, timed entry, ticket, table booking or car hire | Kind, title, provider, start and end time, place, cost, notes. |
| + Meal | A restaurant or food stop | Name, meal type, cuisine, price range, why it's recommended. Give it a **Reservation time** only if a table is booked. |
| + Contact or note | A phone number or tip for the whole day | A contact (name, phone, email) or an info note (title and text). |
| Section buttons | The day's write-up | **Phase** (a part of the day, e.g. "Morning — check out"), **Free text**, **Callout** (a tip or warning), **Choose your adventure** (options to pick from), **Logistics table**, **Travel** (a group for the day's legs). |

Everything you add appears under **On this day**, in the same order as on the trip page. Each item has its own **Confirmations** and **Contacts & info** links for booking numbers and phone numbers.

Items with a time (legs, activities, booked meals) go on the day's timeline in time order. Meals without a reservation time appear under **Food ideas**.

## Link a meal or section to the catalogue

A meal can name its catalogue **Place** (in the Meals form), and a section a **Place** or an **Activity** (on the section's page). The lists offer only places and activities in the trip's cities, so add the city to the trip first. It's optional: most meals stay plain text, and only notable restaurants are in the catalogue. A linked item shows the catalogue name as a link on the trip page and as plain text in the booklet.

When a meal's name or a section's title is the name of one catalogue place or activity in the trip's cities (capitals, punctuation and accents don't matter, and German ö, ä, ü may be written oe, ae, ue), **On this day** shows **Looks like … — Link / Not this one**. Nothing is linked until you click **Link**; **Not this one** stops suggesting it.

## Check it on the trip page and in the booklet

Open the trip page. Your day appears in three places:

- **Overview:** a row with its date, city, where you sleep, travel and highlights. Click the day's number to jump to it.
- **Days:** the day itself, folded. Click its title to open it and check the timeline, write-up and food ideas.
- **Download PDF:** the printed booklet gives the day its own section, with its stay, legs, activities and meals.

## Tips and troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Day N already exists on this trip." | Pick an unused day number. Each day of a trip needs its own number. |
| No **Edit trip** button | You have view-only access. Ask the trip's owner to make you an editor. |
| The city you want isn't in the list | Add the city to the trip on Edit trip first, then come back to the day. |
| A train or flight shows on the wrong day | Edit the leg and correct its date. A leg appears on the day whose date matches its departure. |
| Need to change a day | Edit trip → Days → **Edit**, change the fields, then click **Save day**. |
| Need to remove a day | On **Edit trip**, in the Days table, click the day's **Delete** link and confirm. This permanently removes the day with its meals and sections; booking tasks that pointed at it are kept. |

Tip: add a **Phase** for each part of the day first (Morning, Afternoon, Evening). Then add the legs, activities and meals with times, so the timeline and the write-up line up.
