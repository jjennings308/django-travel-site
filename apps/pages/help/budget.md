# The budget and splitting costs

Click **Budget** at the top of the trip page. It has two parts: the **Plan** (what the trip should cost, what's booked and what's been paid, from the costs on the trip's items) and **Spent** (the costs you record as you go, and who owes whom). Anyone who can see the trip can read it; editors add and change costs.

## The plan

The Plan adds up everything on the trip that counts, in one currency (pick it with **In**; it starts in yours, set under Settings), by category: **Travel**, **Stays**, **Activities & tickets**, **Food** and **Other**. Each row is one thing:

- a journey, stay, activity or meal with its estimate ([what a cost means](#what-a-cost-means)): its total, and each person's share;
- a booking with a cost, once over everything it covers ("GCH7JS · 4 journeys"); the estimates of the things it covers aren't added as well;
- a booking that covers no single thing, such as travel insurance, under Other;
- a chosen option's own cost;
- the **daily food allowance** (below).

Journeys, stays and activities with no cost say **No plan yet**; a meal without a cost isn't listed (the allowance covers it). Something marked **No booking needed** is still in the plan if it has a cost (the €8 museum at the door). An undecided choice shows its options' range, such as "€840–€1,240", and isn't counted until you pick one. Click a row to open it on the trip page, where its cost is changed; ⓘ shows how its total was worked out.

Each category, and the whole trip, shows **Planned** (booked where it's booked, else the estimate), **of which booked**, **Paid** (the costs recorded under Spent, by their category: flights and local transport are Travel, lodging is Stays) and **Left to pay** (planned minus paid, never below zero). On a phone the categories fold; tap one to open it. Below them, each traveller's **share of the plan** sits next to what they've **paid so far**. A cost given for a number of people ("for 4") rather than named ones is added up separately.

Each row also shows what's been **paid** for it: the costs under Spent whose **For** is that booking or one of the things it covers. On a booked row, editors have **Record a payment**: it fills in **Add a cost** below with the booking's description, cost, currency and category, and For set to it, ready for who paid and when. A cost recorded without For still counts in its category, as before.

Amounts in other currencies are converted at the latest rates (or your own rates); one with no rate is listed and left out.

## Daily food

Most meals aren't worth pricing one by one. In **Settings**, **Daily food** is an amount per person per day (with its currency) for the food you haven't priced. The plan adds it for each day and the people on it: "€45 per person per day × 11 days × 6 = €2,970". Days marked **meals included** are skipped. Meals you do price are added as well, so leave the allowance for the rest.

## Adding a cost

**For** (optional) is what the cost paid for: a booking, or a journey, stay, activity or meal of the trip. The Plan then shows it as paid on that row, and the list of costs says "for GCH7JS".


You need [editor access](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what).

1. Under **Add a cost**, fill in what it was, the category, the **Amount** and its **Currency** (as paid: EUR in Munich, GBP in London).
2. Pick the **Date**, who **Paid by**, and who it's **Split between**. Leave **Split between** empty to split it between everyone on the trip.
3. Click **Add cost**.

Costs are split equally. If a cost doesn't divide exactly, the odd cents go to whoever paid.

## Currency choices

Every **Currency** on a trip (costs, stays, journeys, activities, confirmations and a decision's options) offers a short list: the trip's **home currency** first, then the local currency of the place involved, such as EUR for a hotel in Munich, or both ends of a flight from Pittsburgh to Frankfurt. Something new starts on the local currency when there's only one, otherwise on the home currency. For anything else, pick **Other…** and type the three-letter code, such as CHF.

The home currency is set on the trip's **Settings** under The trip. A new trip starts with the currency in your Settings.

## Who owes whom

For each currency, the budget shows the total, what each person paid, their share and their balance (green: they're owed; red: they owe), and **Settle up**: the fewest payments that square everyone.

## Totals in one currency

A trip in several countries ends up with costs in several currencies. **Show totals in** (above the totals) converts every cost into one currency, at the exchange rate for the day it was spent, and shows:

- **≈ Total** for all costs, with **Rates used** (for example "1 EUR = 1.08 USD (ECB, Sep 17)");
- the converted amount (**≈ USD 108.00**) under each cost.

The rates are the European Central Bank's daily reference rates. A cost dated after the latest rate (or with no date) uses the latest one, marked **(latest)**. A cost in a currency the bank doesn't publish is listed as **Not converted** and left out of the total, never guessed.

**Settle up** stays in each currency, since that's how the money was actually spent. Tick **Settle in USD** (or whichever currency you chose) to settle everything in that one currency at those rates instead.

## Your own exchange rate

If you changed money at a known rate and want the budget to use it: on the **Budget** page, under **Your own rates**, add a row such as "1 EUR is worth 1.09 USD" and click **Save rates**. Everyone on the trip sees the rates; editors change them. That rate is used for this trip, both ways, instead of the bank's.

## What a cost means

Every journey, stay, activity, meal and choice option can carry a cost: what you expect to pay, whether or not it needs booking (the €8 castle museum at the door, lunch, the U-Bahn). Next to it, **Cost is** says what the number means, and the site works out the total and each person's share:

| Cost is | Means | Offered on |
|---|---|---|
| **Total** | the whole thing, for everyone it's for | journeys, stays, activities, meals, options |
| **Per person** | each person pays this | journeys, activities, meals, options |
| **Per night** | the whole stay, per night | stays and stay options |
| **Per room per night** | one room, per night; give **Rooms** | stays and stay options |
| **Per person per night** | each person, per night | stays and stay options |

Leave **Cost is** blank and it keeps the usual meaning: the total for a journey or activity, per night for a stay, per person for a meal.

**People** is how many it's for when that isn't everyone: a train for 4 of the 6. Left blank, it's the people on the activity, else the people on that day, else everyone on the trip. If the trip has no travellers yet, the total shows without a share and says "people not set".

Under the fields a line shows the sum as you type, the same as everywhere else the cost appears. For a stay for six in Germany, compare:

- three hotel rooms: "€95 per room per night × 3 rooms × 4 nights = €1,140 · €190 each (6)";
- one 3-bedroom flat: "€310 per night × 4 nights = €1,240 · €206.67 each (6)".

In a "choose one" box, options priced this way show the same line, so they compare directly, and each option shows its total and its cost per person ("EUR 380.00 · EUR 190.00 pp"). When two or more options are priced for the same number of people, the box ends with **Cheapest per person**. **Pick** carries the cost and its meaning onto the stay, meal, activity or journey it makes.

On the trip page, each journey, stay, activity and meal with a cost shows it quietly at the end of its row: "≈ €190 pp" from its estimate, or "€1,140 · booked" once a booking with a cost covers it ("€380 for 4 · booked" when the booking covers several things). The day's summary line and the booklet don't show costs.

## Estimated trip cost

The trip page shows **Estimated trip cost** under **About this trip**, above the days: the Plan's total and how much of it is booked ("≈ USD 14,200 planned · USD 6,100 booked"), in your currency, linking to the Budget page. It's the same sum as the Plan, so the two always agree.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Not converted (no rate)" | The currency has no ECB rate. Add your own on the Budget page under **Your own rates**. |
| A total looks off by a little | Each cost uses its own day's rate, so the same amount on different days converts differently. |
| No **Add a cost** form | Only editors add costs. |
