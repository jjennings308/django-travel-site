# Places, activities and events

The catalogue is what everyone's goals and trips are built from. An **activity** is an idea ("See Oktoberfest"), an **event** is one dated occurrence of it ("Oktoberfest 2027"), and a **place** is somewhere on the map.

## Browse

Under **Discover** in the left menu: **Activities**, **Events** and **Places**. Each has filters, and places also have a map. A place or activity that's part of several members' trips shows **On trips** with how many (from two trips up; never which trips or whose). On anything you like, click **Add to bucket list** ([your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/)).

## Suggest something new

- **Add an activity** on the Activities page.
- **Add an event** on the Events page, or **Add a date** on an activity, for its next occurrence. If the city isn't listed, tick **City not listed** and type it.
- **Add POI** on the Places list, for a new place. A restaurant, market or bakery belongs here only if people travel for it (type **Food & drink**); everyday meals stay on your trip as typed text.

New suggestions are checked by the site's staff before others see them. Until then you can see and edit yours under **My contributions** in your account menu (**Activities I added**, **Events I added**). You get a notification when one is approved.

## Reviews

On a published activity, event, city or place, click **Write a review**, give it a rating and some words, and click **Post review**. You can write one review per item; **Edit your review** changes it, and **Delete review** removes it. Reviews are public and show your username. A review you share when ticking off a goal is marked **Been there**.

If a review breaks the rules, click **Report**, say **What's wrong with it?** and click **Send report**. Staff decide whether to keep it.

## Rating a finished trip

The day after a trip ends, everyone on it gets a notification: **How was <the trip>?** Open it (or, on the finished trip's page, click **Rate the places from this trip**) to see the trip's cities and the places its activities visited.

Give any of them 1 to 5 stars and a sentence if you like, then click **Save ratings**. Each rating is your review of that place, the same one you'd write on its page; rating it again here changes your review. If you travelled (you're on the trip's list of travellers), the review is marked **Been there**. Leave out anything you'd rather not rate, or click **Skip — don't ask again**.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| My suggestion doesn't show for others | It's waiting for approval. Check **My contributions**. |
| The city I need isn't listed | Tick **City not listed** and type it; staff add it to the catalogue. |
| No **Write a review** | Log in. Items still waiting for approval can't be reviewed yet. |
