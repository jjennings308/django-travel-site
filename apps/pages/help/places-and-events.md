# Places, activities and events

The catalogue is what everyone's goals and trips are built from. An **activity** is an idea ("See Oktoberfest"), an **event** is one dated occurrence of it ("Oktoberfest 2027"), and a **place** is somewhere on the map.

## Browse

Under **Discover** in the left menu: **Activities**, **Events** and **Places** — open to everyone, signed in or not, so you can share a link to any published one. Each has filters, and places also have a map. A place, activity or event that's part of several members' trips shows **On trips** with how many (from two trips up; never which trips or whose). On anything you like, click **Add to bucket list** ([your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/)).

## Searching

The **search box** at the top of every page looks through the whole catalogue at once: countries and regions, cities, places, activities and events. On a phone, tap the magnifier.

- Type a place (*Kyoto*, *Bavaria*), something to do (*food tour*, *museum*) or an event (*Oktoberfest*). Small spelling slips and missing accents are fine: *Neuschwanstien* and *Munchen* both work.
- Results are grouped by kind, best match first. A name match comes before a match on where something is, which comes before a match in its description. **More …** shows all of one kind.
- Narrow it with **Country**, **Month** (when an event starts, or the months an activity usually happens), **Category** and **Show**. A month or category leaves only activities and events.
- Past events are left out unless you tick **Include past events**.

Only published items are found; your own suggestions waiting for approval show up for you alone.

## What's on and the calendar

- **Calendar:** on the Events page, click **Calendar** for a month at a glance. An event shows on every day it runs; a busy day says "+N more", which jumps to that day's list. Use the arrows for other months and the filters for a country or category. On a phone it's a list of the days with something on.
- **What's on:** country, region, city and place pages list what's coming up there. A city also shows events within 50 km, marked **Nearby** with the distance; a place (a stadium, a hall) shows the events held there. **Things to do** lists activities in the same place.
- **Add to my calendar:** on an event's page, this downloads a file your calendar app (Apple, Google, Outlook) opens to add the event, with its times in the event's own time zone.

Dates and "today" follow the time zone in your **Settings**.

## Suggest something new

- **Add an activity** on the Activities page.
- **Add an event** on the Events page, or **Add a date** on an activity, for its next occurrence. If the city isn't listed, tick **City not listed** and type it.
- **Add POI** on the Places list, for a new place. A restaurant, market or bakery belongs here only if people travel for it (type **Food & drink**); everyday meals stay on your trip as typed text.

New suggestions are checked by the site's staff before others see them. Until then you can see and edit yours under **My contributions** in your account menu (**Activities I added**, **Events I added**). You get a notification when one is approved.

## Good to know notes

A place, activity or event page has a **Good to know** card: things a traveller should know before going, such as "Cash only; no ATM near the castle", or "Timed entry: book 3 months ahead". An event also shows its activity's notes and its venue's (marked "From …"), and an activity its venue's.

To add one, click **Add a note**:

- **Tone**: Tip, Warning or Critical.
- **Text**: what to know, in a sentence or two.
- **Closed on**: tick the days it's closed every week, if any.
- **Book ahead** and **Booking link**: how far ahead to book (for example 3 months), and where.
- **Last checked**: when you confirmed it; it shows as "checked Oct 2026", so readers know how fresh it is.

Staff check each note before others see it; until then only you can, marked "waiting for review", and you can still edit or delete it. If a published note looks wrong or out of date, click **Out of date?** under it and say what's changed. Trips that include the place, activity or event will show its notes.

## Reviews

On a published activity, event, city or place, click **Write a review**, give it a rating and some words, and click **Post review**. You can write one review per item; **Edit your review** changes it, and **Delete review** removes it. Reviews are public and show your username. A review you share when ticking off a goal is marked **Been there**.

If a review breaks the rules, click **Report**, say **What's wrong with it?** and click **Send report**. Staff decide whether to keep it.

## Rating a finished trip

The day after a trip ends, everyone on it gets a notification: **How was <the trip>?** Open it (or, on the finished trip's page, click **Rate the places from this trip**) to see the trip's cities and the places its activities visited.

Give any of them 1 to 5 stars and a sentence if you like, then click **Save ratings**. Each rating is your review of that place, the same one you'd write on its page; rating it again here changes your review. If you travelled (you're on the trip's list of travellers), the review is marked **Been there**. Leave out anything you'd rather not rate, or click **Skip — don't ask again**.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| My suggestion doesn't show for others | It's waiting for approval. Check **My contributions**. |
| The city I need isn't listed | Tick **City not listed** and type it; staff add it to the catalogue. |
| No **Write a review** | Log in. Items still waiting for approval can't be reviewed yet. |
