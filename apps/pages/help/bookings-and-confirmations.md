# Bookings and confirmations

A confirmation number belongs to the thing it confirms: a train or flight, a stay, an activity, a meal or one of the day's plans. You add it there, and the trip page shows it in [Reference](/help/reading-your-trip/#reference) with everything it covers. You need [editor access](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what).

## Add a confirmation number

1. Open the thing it confirms:
    - a train or flight: click **Edit** beside it on the trip page;
    - an activity: click **Edit** beside it, or open it from its day's page;
    - a stay: click **Edit** on its card on the trip page, or on its row on its day's page; the stay's page has the **Confirmations** box (and **Contacts & info** for the hotel's phone);
    - a meal or one of the day's plans: open the day with **Edit this day**, then click **Confirmations** on its row.
2. In the **Confirmations** box, type the **Confirmation number**, and the **Provider** if it's new (for example Delta).
3. Click **Add confirmation**.

The number now shows as a chip on the item, and in full in Reference. To change its provider, label, manage-booking link, cost, booking date, notes or who's on it, click **Edit details** next to it.

## One number for several things

Many bookings cover more than one thing: one flight booking for four flights, one rail ticket for two trains. Add the **same number** to each of them. The site recognises a number the trip already has (ignoring spaces and capitals) and links it, rather than making a second copy, so Reference lists it once with everything it covers.

You can also pick it from **Or pick one this trip has** and click **Add**.

To take a number off one item, click **Remove from this**. The confirmation stays on the trip and on its other items.

## Tickets and documents

Boarding passes, e-tickets and booking PDFs go on the confirmation they belong to, so everyone on the trip can open them from their phone.

1. Open the confirmation: click **Edit details** next to its number in an item's **Confirmations** box, or **Edit** on its row in Reference.
2. Under **Tickets & documents**, choose one or more files and click **Upload**. In an item's **Confirmations** box, **Add a file** does the same.

You can add PDF, PNG and JPEG files and iPhone photos (stored as JPEG), up to 10 MB each and 200 MB for the whole trip. Tickets also count towards your own 1 GB for photos and tickets ([storage](/help/reporting-and-safety/#storage)). Anything else is turned away with a message saying why.

On the trip page, a confirmation with files shows **📎** and how many on its chip, and its row in Reference lists them; click a file to open it. Anyone who can see the trip can open them; only editors add or remove them (**Remove** next to the file). Deleting a confirmation deletes its files too. The PDF booklet lists the file names under each confirmation, but doesn't include the files.

## A journey's stages

Each train or flight shows its stage:

| Stage | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Planned** | It has a day but no times yet, perhaps just a rough "~45 min". |
| **Scheduled** | It has departure and arrival times. |
| **Booked** | It has at least one confirmation number. |

## Insurance and passes

A confirmation that covers no single item, such as travel insurance or a rail pass, is a loose one. On **Edit trip**, under **Loose confirmations**, click **Add a loose confirmation**. It shows in Reference under Other.

## What's still to book

Reference keeps two lists:

- **Still to book**: the trip's tasks, such as "ICE seats for 6", most urgent first. A task can be about one journey, stay or activity; it then shows on that item as **Still to book**. If a task's name mentions a stay, journey or activity, editors see **Looks like:** with a **Link** button to connect them, or **Not this one**.
- **Not booked yet**: flights, rental cars, trains with an operator or number, stays and bookable activities (tours, timed entries, tickets, tables, car hire) that have no confirmation and no task. Nothing needs typing to appear here.

When you add the first confirmation to something that has open tasks, the site asks **Mark these done?** Tick the tasks that are finished and click **Mark ticked done**, or click **Not yet**.

## The To do page

**To do** lists every task on the trip: open ones by priority and due date, then the finished ones folded under **Done**. Each says what it's for ("For: Day 4 · Munich → Salzburg") and a decision shows its options. Open it from **N to do** in the **Needs attention** banner, or **All to-dos** beside **Still to book** in Reference. Anyone who can see the trip can read it.

Editors use **Add a to-do** there, and **Edit**, **Mark done** (or **Not done yet**), **Options** and **Delete** on each one. To add a task about one journey, stay or activity, open that item's page and click **Add a to-do for this** under **Still to book**: the task is already linked to it. Its **Still to book** lines open the task.

## To-dos from catalogue notes

When a place's note says to book ahead, such as Arches' timed entry 3 months ahead, the trip shows **Book by <date>** under the item. Click **Add as a to-do**: a to-do opens filled in with "Book ahead: <place>", the book-by date as its due date, and the booking link. Save it, and the trip page says "a to-do covers it" instead of warning you.

## Decisions with options

Choosing between options is now a **choice** on a day, which everyone can vote on and an editor picks ([choices, options and votes](/help/options-and-votes/)); new to-dos are bookings. The To do page lists undecided choices at the top. A decision to-do from before still works as below, and **Needs attention** offers to convert it into a choice.

Some tasks are a choice, such as which hotel to book. Make the task a decision by giving it options:

1. In Reference, under the task, click **Add options** (or **Options (n)** once it has some).
2. For each option, fill in its title, and if you like a detail, a link and an estimated cost.
3. Tick **Chosen** on the one you pick, or **Not decided yet**, and click **Save**.

The task now reads **Decide:** with its options, the chosen one ticked. Choosing doesn't book or add anything. To add the choice to the trip, use **Add as a stay** (opens a new stay filled in, on the task's day or else Day 1; change its dates there) or **Add as an activity** (pick a day; opens a new activity filled in). Nothing is saved until you save that page.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The same number shows twice in Reference | One copy was typed with a different spelling. Open one and fix its number, or remove it from its items and delete it. |
| A train still says **Scheduled** | It has no confirmation yet. Add its number. |
| Something is in **Not booked yet** that is booked | Add its confirmation number, or a task if it's being handled. |
| No **Confirmations** box | You need editor access. See [sharing and access](/help/sharing-and-access/). |
| A file won't upload | It must be a PDF, PNG, JPEG or iPhone photo, at most 10 MB, and the trip's files can't go over 200 MB in total. Remove old ones, or save a large scan at lower quality. |
