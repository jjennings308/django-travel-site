# Bookings and confirmations

A confirmation number belongs to the thing it confirms: a train or flight, a stay, an activity, a meal or one of the day's plans. You add it there, and the trip page shows it in [Reference](/help/reading-your-trip/#reference) with everything it covers. You need [editor access](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what).

## Add a confirmation number

1. Open the thing it confirms:
    - a train or flight: click **Edit** beside it on the trip page;
    - an activity: click **Edit** beside it, or open it from its day's page;
    - a stay: click **Edit** on its card on the trip page, or on its row on its day's page; the stay's page has the **Confirmations** box (and **Contacts & info** for the hotel's phone);
    - a meal or one of the day's plans: open the day with **Edit this day**, then click **Confirmations** on its row.
2. In the **Confirmations** box, type the **Confirmation number**, and the **Provider** if it's new (for example Delta).
3. Click **Add confirmation**.

The number now shows as a chip on the item, and in full in Reference. To change its provider, label, manage-booking link, cost, booking date, notes or who's on it, click **Edit details** next to it.

## One number for several things

Many bookings cover more than one thing: one flight booking for four flights, one rail ticket for two trains. Add the **same number** to each of them. The site recognises a number the trip already has (ignoring spaces and capitals) and links it, rather than making a second copy, so Reference lists it once with everything it covers.

You can also pick it from **Or pick one this trip has** and click **Add**.

To take a number off one item, click **Remove from this**. The confirmation stays on the trip and on its other items.

## Tickets and documents

Boarding passes, e-tickets and booking PDFs go on the confirmation they belong to, so everyone on the trip can open them from their phone.

1. Open the confirmation: click **Edit details** next to its number in an item's **Confirmations** box, or **Edit** on its row in Reference.
2. Under **Tickets & documents**, choose one or more files and click **Upload**. In an item's **Confirmations** box, **Add a file** does the same.

You can add PDF, PNG and JPEG files and iPhone photos (stored as JPEG), up to 10 MB each and 200 MB for the whole trip. Tickets also count towards your own 1 GB for photos and tickets ([storage](/help/reporting-and-safety/#storage)). Anything else is turned away with a message saying why.

On the trip page, a confirmation with files shows **📎** and how many on its chip, and its row in Reference lists them; click a file to open it. Anyone who can see the trip can open them; only editors add or remove them (**Remove** next to the file). Deleting a confirmation deletes its files too. The PDF booklet lists the file names under each confirmation, but doesn't include the files.

## A journey's stages

Each train or flight shows its stage:

| Stage | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Planned** | It has a day but no times yet, perhaps just a rough "~45 min". |
| **Scheduled** | It has departure and arrival times. |
| **Booked** | It has at least one confirmation number. |

## Insurance and passes

A confirmation that covers no single item, such as travel insurance or a rail pass, is a loose one. On the trip's **Settings**, under **Loose confirmations**, click **Add a loose confirmation**. It shows in Reference under Other.

## To book

Reference's **To book** list is everything on the trip still to book, in date order:

- each journey, stay and activity with no confirmation that usually needs booking (flights, rental cars, trains with an operator or number, stays, tours, timed entries, tickets, tables and car hire) or that has a to-do. Under it is its to-do, such as "Book trains · High · due Aug 1", or **No plan yet** when it has none. Things one to-do covers together are one row: "Augsburg → Munich and back";
- then **Other to-dos**: to-dos that aren't about a journey, stay or activity, such as "Travel insurance". If a task's name mentions a stay, journey or activity, editors see **Looks like:** with a **Link** button to add it, or **Not this one**.

A row works like an item on a day: click it and it opens beside the days (an editor gets the to-do or the item to edit; anyone else a read-only view). Editors also have, on each row:

- **Mark done** on a to-do: the row greys and moves to **Done today** (it's gone the next time you open the page);
- on a **No plan yet** row: **Add a to-do** (it already covers the item, and a journey's way back and connections), **Add confirmation**, or **No booking needed**.

After a save beside the days, the list is refreshed. **Needs attention** counts the rows ("3 to book") and the other to-dos.

### No booking needed

Some things never need booking: a walk-up regional train, a free museum, a stay with friends. Click **No booking needed** on its row, or tick **No booking needed** on its page (under Details for a journey). It then never shows in To book, Not booked yet or the days' ⚠, and its row on the day says "no booking needed" instead of Planned or Scheduled. Untick it on its page to put it back.

When a confirmation makes everything a task covers confirmed, the site asks **Mark these done?** Tick the tasks that are finished and click **Mark ticked done**, or click **Not yet**.

## One to-do for several things

One booking often covers more than one thing: a return train ticket is two journeys, a flight with a connection is two legs (PIT → ATL, ATL → FRA), a rail pass covers several trains, a hotel may come with its airport transfer. One to-do covers them all. On the to-do's page, **Covers** lists the trip's journeys, stays and activities by day: tick each one it's for. The to-do then shows on every one of them, and none of them is listed under **Not booked yet**. It reads as one line: "For: Augsburg → Munich and back", "PIT → ATL → FRA", or "Hotel Laimer Hof + 1 more" (point at it to see the rest).

**Add a to-do for this** on a journey's page ticks that journey and also its way back and the legs it connects with that day; the page says what it ticked ("Also ticked: Munich → Augsburg (the way back)"). Untick any that are booked separately.

When you add a confirmation number to one of them and the to-do covers others with no confirmation yet, the site asks **Also attach GCH7JS to:** with each of them ticked. Click **Attach to the ticked ones**, or **Not these**. Nothing is attached without asking.

When you add a return trip on a journey's page, **Same booking as the way out** puts the return on the way out's open to-dos and confirmations. It's ticked when the way out has either; untick it when the return is booked on its own ([return trips](/help/travel-legs/#return-trips)).

## The To do page

**To do** (at the top of the trip page) answers "what do I book next?": choices still to make (**Decide:** …), then every task on the trip — open ones by priority and due date, then the finished ones folded under **Done** — then **No plan yet**, the things to book that no to-do covers yet (with **Add a to-do**, **Add confirmation** and **No booking needed**). Each thing is listed once. **Mark done** ticks a to-do off where it is. Each says what it's for ("For: Day 4 · Munich → Salzburg"). Open it from **All to-dos** beside **To book** in Reference. Anyone who can see the trip can read it.

Editors use **Add a to-do** there, and **Edit**, **Mark done** (or **Not done yet**) and **Delete** on each one. To add a task about a journey, stay or activity, open that item's page and click **Add a to-do for this** under **Still to book**: the task already covers it (and, for a journey, its way back and connections). Its **Still to book** lines open the task.

## The Bookings page

**Bookings**, at the top of the trip page, is the trip's bookings in one place, for everyone who can see the trip: the confirmations (grouped as in Reference), **Not booked yet** (the things to book with no plan yet), and **Getting around & stays**, every journey and stay in date order with its day and confirmation numbers (a journey or stay whose date is outside the trip's days is flagged). Nothing is changed on it: click a line and it opens on the trip page, beside the days, where editors change it. The same goes for the lines on **To do**, the map page and the [calendar feed](/help/calendar-feed/).

## To-dos from catalogue notes

When a place's note says to book ahead, such as Arches' timed entry 3 months ahead, the trip shows **Book by <date>** under the item. Click **Add as a to-do**: a to-do opens filled in with "Book ahead: <place>", the book-by date as its due date, and the booking link. Save it, and the trip page says "a to-do covers it" instead of warning you.

## Choosing between options

Choosing between options, such as which hotel to book, is a **choice** on a day: everyone can vote, and an editor picks one ([choices, options and votes](/help/options-and-votes/)). The To do page lists undecided choices at the top. To-dos are for booking and don't have options ([from older trips](/help/options-and-votes/#from-older-trips)).

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The same number shows twice in Reference | One copy was typed with a different spelling. Open one and fix its number, or remove it from its items and delete it. |
| A train still says **Scheduled** | It has no confirmation yet. Add its number. |
| Something is in **Not booked yet** that is booked | Add its confirmation number, or a task if it's being handled. |
| No **Confirmations** box | You need editor access. See [sharing and access](/help/sharing-and-access/). |
| A file won't upload | It must be a PDF, PNG, JPEG or iPhone photo, at most 10 MB, and the trip's files can't go over 200 MB in total. Remove old ones, or save a large scan at lower quality. |
