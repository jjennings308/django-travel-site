# Checklists

A trip can have checklists: what to pack, which documents everyone needs, what to do before you leave. They're in **Reference** at the bottom of the trip page, and in the PDF booklet as tick boxes.

## Ticking things off

On the trip page, under **Reference** → **Checklists**, click a box to tick it; click again to untick. Anyone who can [comment on the trip](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what) can tick; viewers see the ticks but can't change them.

Some items are for **each person**, such as a passport: they show one box per traveller ("Passport — ☑ James ☐ Regan"), and the item counts as done once everyone has ticked theirs. Other items are done once for the whole group, such as plug adapters.

From two weeks before the trip until it ends, the **Needs attention** banner at the top of the trip page counts the boxes still empty on **Documents** checklists, such as "3 documents not ticked".

## Making a checklist

You need editor access.

1. Click **Settings** at the top of the trip page and go to **Checklists** at the bottom.
2. Either pick one under **Start from a template** and click **Add it**, or click **New checklist**.
3. Give it a title and a kind (Packing, Documents, Before you go or Other), and fill in the items. Tick **Each person** for things every traveller does themselves.
4. Click **Save**. Save again to get more empty rows; tick **Remove** to take an item off.

The templates are a starting point:

| Template | For |
| --- | --- |
| International trip | Passports, visas or ETIAS, insurance, cards, phone plans and adapters |
| Rail trip | Passes, seat reservations, tickets and checking for strikes |
| Road trip | The car, licences, tolls and offline maps |
| Packing basics | Medicines, chargers, shoes, rain gear and the other easy-to-forget things |

Change any template list as you like once it's added. **Delete this checklist** at the bottom of its page removes it and its ticks.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No box for someone on an "each person" item | They're not on the trip's travellers list. An editor adds them on the trip's **Settings**. |
| Clicking a box does nothing | You can read the trip but not comment on it. Ask an editor to make you a commentor. |
| No **Checklists** section on the trip page | The trip has none yet. An editor adds one on the trip's **Settings**. |
