# Contacts and info notes

A **contact** is someone to reach: a hotel's front desk, a taxi firm, a tour guide. An **info note** is know-how: ticket rules, a tent guide, what to pack. Each lives once in the trip's [Reference](/help/reading-your-trip/#reference), and you link it to the things it matters for, so it also shows right where it's needed.

## Where a linked contact or note shows

| Linked to | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| A train, flight, activity or meal | Right under it on its day: a **☎** line with a phone number you can tap, or an **ⓘ** line you tap to read. |
| A stay | On the stay's card, and on the **Sleeping:** line of every night there. |
| A plan section, or a whole day | In the day's **Good to know**. |
| Anything | In full in Reference (**Contacts** or **Info**), with **On:** links back to each place it's linked. |

The booklet prints each one in full on the days it applies to, and again in its Reference pages. A shared link never shows them.

## Add one from the thing it's for

On the item's page (a stay's, leg's or activity's **Edit** page), or from the **Contacts & info** links on a day's page, use the **Contacts & info** box:

- **Pick one this trip has** and click **Add**, to link one you've already made;
- or fill in **New contact** (name, role, phone, email) and click **Add contact**;
- or fill in **New info note** (title, text) and click **Add note**.

For something about the whole day, use **+ Contact or note** at the top of the day's page.

**Remove from this** unlinks it from the item; the contact or note itself is kept.

## Their own pages

On **Edit trip**, under **Contacts & info**, click **New contact** or **New info note**, or **Edit** beside one. A contact has **Name**, **Role**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Address**, **Website** and **Notes**; an info note has a **Title**, a **Body** (paragraphs separated by a blank line), a **Link** and an **Order**.

Under **Show it on**, tick every item and day it matters for. Untick to unlink. Click **Save**.

**Delete** removes it from Reference and from everything it was on; the items themselves are kept.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A phone number isn't tappable | Put it in the contact's **Phone** field, not in its notes. |
| A contact shows on the wrong day | Check what it's linked to under **Show it on**. |
| A phone number is typed in a day's text | Use [Tidy suggestions](/help/tidying-an-itinerary/#clean-up-suggestions) to turn it into a contact. |
