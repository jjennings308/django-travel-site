# Ideas and goals on a trip

Not everything you'd like to do on a trip has a day yet. The trip's **Ideas** shelf holds those things: "Day trip to Salzburg", "Augustiner Keller", "Oktoberfest 2027". Everyone on the trip sees the shelf and can vote; editors move ideas onto the days.

## The Ideas shelf

On the trip page, **Ideas** is the fold after **About this trip**, with how many are waiting ("Ideas · 7"). Each idea shows:

- what it is, and a link to the catalogue place, activity, event or city it's about, when it has one;
- its notes, who added it, and whose bucket-list goal it is ("🎯 Debbie's goal") when it came from one;
- **👍** and how many people voted for it, with their names.

An idea leaves the shelf once it's on a day or in an option. The shared read-only link never shows ideas.

## Adding ideas

Anyone who can comment on the trip can add ideas ([who can do what](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what)):

- **Type one** at the bottom of the shelf, choose what kind of thing it is if you know (something to do, somewhere to eat, a stay, travel), and click **Add idea**.
- **From the catalogue**: on a place, activity, event or city page, click **Add to a trip** and pick one of your trips ([places and events](/help/places-and-events/)). It goes on that trip's shelf, linked to the catalogue page. Adding the same thing twice keeps one idea.
- **From your bucket list**: putting a goal on a trip makes it an idea there (see [below](#bucket-list-goals)).

Click **Edit** on an idea you added to change it or remove it. Editors can change or remove any idea.

## Voting

Click **👍** on an idea you'd like to do. Click it again to take your vote back. A bucket-list goal on an idea counts as its owner's vote.

## Putting an idea on a day

Editors choose a day and what it becomes, then click **Put on this day**. The kind starts as the idea's own, or as the catalogue item suggests: an activity or event is something to do, a Food & drink place somewhere to eat. The new item opens so you can add its time and details:

| It becomes | Filled in from the idea |
|---|---|
| Do (an activity) | the title, notes, the catalogue place (or the activity's or event's venue) and the event |
| Eat (a meal) | the name, notes and the catalogue place |
| Stay | the name and city, from the day it starts |
| Travel (a journey) | a planned journey to the idea's city |
| Note | the title and notes, linked to the catalogue item |

The place's city joins the trip's cities. Delete the item later and the idea goes back on the shelf.

## Making an idea an option

When you haven't decided yet, choose **Option of "Afternoon" (Day 4)** for an undecided choice, or **New choice on Day 4**, and click **Add as an option**. The idea becomes an option travellers can vote on ([choices, options and votes](/help/options-and-votes/)). Delete the option, or the choice, and the idea goes back on the shelf.

Only choices the idea fits are listed: a choice about where to stay takes stay ideas, one about where to eat takes Food & drink places and eat ideas, and so on ([what a choice is about](/help/options-and-votes/#what-a-choice-is-about)); a choice about **Anything** takes any idea. A new choice made from an idea is about the same kind of thing. The idea's notes and catalogue link go into the option, so it shows its place and, when you pick it, becomes the stay, meal or activity there. On an **Anything** choice an idea linked to the catalogue brings its item into the option instead, as before.

## Bucket-list goals

When you put a goal on a trip (**Plan a trip** or **Add to this trip** on the goal, or **Add one of your bucket-list goals** on the shelf), it becomes an idea on the trip's shelf, linked to the same catalogue item as the goal. Your personal notes stay private: only the goal's title and catalogue link are copied. If someone else's goal is about the same thing, you share one idea and it shows both: "Debbie's and your goals".

If you can only read the trip, the goal is linked to the trip but no idea is added.

When the trip is over, **Tick it off** offers the goals whose idea made it onto the trip: put on a day, or in an option that was picked or taken in a split. Goals still on the shelf, or in an option nobody took, aren't offered.

## Save for someday

**Save for someday** on an idea, or on an option under **Considered**, adds it to your own bucket list for another time: the catalogue item it's about, else its title. If it's already on your list, it says so and adds nothing.

## On the map

The trip's **Map** ([the top of the page](/help/reading-your-trip/#the-top-of-the-page)) has an **Ideas** switch, off at first: it shows ideas on the shelf that have a place as faint pins. A **Choices** switch does the same for the options of undecided choices.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **Add idea** box | You can read the trip but not comment. Ask an editor for the commentor role. |
| No **Put on this day** | Only editors put ideas on days. Vote for it instead. |
| **Add to a trip** lists no trips | It lists trips that haven't ended and that you can comment on. |
