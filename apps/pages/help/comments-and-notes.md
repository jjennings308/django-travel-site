# Comments

Comments are a conversation between the people planning a trip. They're not part of the itinerary: the booklet and a shared link never show them. (A **Note** you add to a day with **+ Add** is different: it's part of the day's plan, and it's printed. See [adding a day](/help/adding-a-day/#notes).)

## Who can comment

Commentors and editors can add comments; viewers can read them ([who can do what](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what)).

## Comments on the trip

At the bottom of the trip page, under **Comments on this trip**, click **Add a comment**, write it and click **Save**.

## Comments on a day, a plan or a task

- **A day:** open the day and click **Add a comment to this day**, at the end of the day.
- **One of the day's plans:** click **Add a comment** under it.
- **A task:** in Reference, under **To book**, click **Add a comment** under the task.

Write your comment and click **Save**. You land back on the trip page at your comment, with its day open.

Once a day, plan or task has comments, they show where they belong (**Comments on this day**, the plan's title with "comments", **Comments on this task**), and **Add a comment** under them adds yours to the conversation. In an item's side panel they're under the **Comments** tab.

## Editing or deleting your comments

Click **Edit** beside your comment. Change it and click **Save**, or click **Delete**; deleting removes it for everyone. An edited comment is marked **(edited)**. You can only edit your own comments; the site's staff can edit any.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Add a comment** | You're a viewer. Ask an editor to make you a commentor ([changing roles](/help/sharing-and-access/#changing-roles-and-removing-people)). |
| No **Edit** on a comment | It isn't yours. |
| A comment isn't in the PDF | Comments are never printed. |
