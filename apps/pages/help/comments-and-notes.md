# Comments and notes

Notes are a conversation between the people planning a trip. They're not part of the itinerary: the booklet and a shared link never show them.

## Who can add notes

Commentors and editors can add notes; viewers can read them ([who can do what](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what)).

## Notes on the trip

At the bottom of the trip page, under **Trip notes**, click **Add a comment**, write your note and click **Save**.

## Notes on a day, a plan or a task

- **A day:** open the day and click **Add a note to this day**, at the end of the day.
- **One of the day's plans:** click **Add a note** under it.
- **A task:** in Reference, under **Still to book**, click **Add a note** under the task.

Write your note and click **Save**. You land back on the trip page at your note, with its day open.

Once a day, plan or task has notes, they show where they belong (**Notes on this day**, the plan's title with "notes", **Notes on this task**), and **Add a comment** under them adds yours to the conversation.

## Editing or deleting your notes

Click **Edit** beside your note. Change it and click **Save**, or click **Delete**; deleting removes it for everyone. An edited note is marked **(edited)**. You can only edit your own notes; the site's staff can edit any.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Add a comment** | You're a viewer. Ask an editor to make you a commentor ([changing roles](/help/sharing-and-access/#changing-roles-and-removing-people)). |
| No **Edit** on a note | It isn't yours. |
| A note isn't in the PDF | Notes are never printed. |
