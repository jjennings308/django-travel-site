# Comments and notes

Notes are a conversation between the people planning a trip. They're not part of the itinerary: the booklet and a shared link never show them.

## Who can add notes

Commentors and editors can add notes; viewers can read them ([who can do what](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what)).

## Notes on the trip

At the bottom of the trip page, under **Trip notes**, click **Add a comment**, write your note and click **Save**.

## Notes on a day, a plan or a task

Notes on a day (**Notes on this day**), on one of its plans (headed with the plan's title and "notes") or on a task in Reference (**Notes on this task**) show right where they belong. When a day, plan or task already has notes, **Add a comment** under them adds yours to the conversation.

## Editing or deleting your notes

Click **Edit** beside your note. Change it and click **Save**, or click **Delete**; deleting removes it for everyone. An edited note is marked **(edited)**. You can only edit your own notes; the site's staff can edit any.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Add a comment** | You're a viewer. Ask for commentor access ([getting access](/help/sharing-and-access/#getting-access)). |
| No **Edit** on a note | It isn't yours. |
| A note isn't in the PDF | Notes are never printed. |
