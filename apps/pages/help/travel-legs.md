# Travel legs

A leg is one journey: a flight, a train, a drive or a walk. It starts as a plan and grows into a booking on the same record.

## Planned, scheduled, booked

| Stage | When | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planned** | It has a day, and perhaps a rough time such as "~45 min", but no times. | Under **Any time** on its day, in the order you give it. |
| **Scheduled** | It has departure and arrival times. | On the day's timeline at its time. |
| **Booked** | It has a confirmation number ([bookings](/help/bookings-and-confirmations/)). | The same, with its number. |

## Add a leg quickly

On a day's page, click **+ Leg** and type the leg like a table row: **From**, **To**, **Mode**, **~Duration** (such as "45 min" or "2.5 hrs") and a **Note**, then click **Add leg**. It's planned, on that day. The site matches the places to the trip's cities by name where it can.

## Edit a leg

Click **Edit** beside the leg on the trip page or on its day's page. The leg's page has these groups:

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Route** | **Origin** and **Destination** (an airport code, station or town), **From city** and **To city** (to draw it on the map), **Mode**. |
| **Schedule** | **Date**; **Departure (local)** and **Arrival (local)** with each end's time zone; **Rough duration**; **Order** (for legs without times). |
| **Details** | **Operator** (airline, rail company), **Service number** (flight or train number), **Cost**, **Currency**, **Notes**. |
| **Placement** | **Shown on its day under**: which travel section of the day it sits in. |
| **Return trip** | **Also add the return trip** ([below](#return-trips)). |

Click **Save**. Below the form are the leg's **Confirmations** and **Contacts & info**.

## Times and time zones

Times are picked (date and time, with your browser's picker) as the clock shows them where the leg leaves (**Departure (local)**) and where it arrives (**Arrival (local)**), each with its place's time zone, such as Europe/Berlin. The trip page shows them the same way, whatever time zone you read it from: a 10:00 train from Frankfurt says 10:00 CEST in New York too.

Leave both times blank while a leg is only planned. Once you add them, the leg's date follows its departure.

## Which day a leg is on

A leg appears on the day it departs. An overnight flight shows on the day it leaves, with "(+1 day)" on its arrival. A leg whose date isn't one of the trip's days is listed on the trip page under **Journeys not on any day**, so it isn't lost: edit its date.

## Return trips

Tick **Also add the return trip** when you save a leg, and give the return's times (or just its day, if it's only planned). The site adds a second leg back the other way, with the ends and time zones swapped and the mode and operator copied. The two legs are separate from then on.

## Travel sections

By default a day's legs are on its timeline in time order. If you'd rather group them under a heading of the day's own, such as "Getting there", add a travel section (the **Travel** button on the day's page) and choose it under **Shown on its day under** on each leg. A travel section can also hold reference rows, such as a ticket's price.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A leg is on the wrong day | Check its **Date**, or its departure time and time zone. |
| The times look hours off | Check each end's time zone. A blank zone means UTC. |
| A leg isn't drawn on the map | Pick its **From city** and **To city**. |
| "Turn into legs" rows came out as the wrong places | Edit each leg's ends; see [tidying an itinerary](/help/tidying-an-itinerary/). |
