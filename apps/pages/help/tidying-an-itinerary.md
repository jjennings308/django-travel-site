# Tidying an itinerary

An itinerary written as a document often lists journeys in tables and repeats bookings in the text. Two tools turn that into the trip's real records. Neither changes anything until you tick and confirm.

## Turn tables into legs

A table on a day with rows like "Butzbach → Frankfurt Hbf" can become real journeys ([travel legs](/help/travel-legs/)).

1. On **Edit trip**, under **Travel by day**, click **Turn “A → B” table rows into legs** (shown while any table has such rows). For just one table, open it from the day's page and click **Turn them into legs**.
2. Each row shows what it will become:
    - **Create planned leg**: a new journey on that day, with the row's rough time, operator and notes.
    - **Link to existing leg**: the day already has this journey; the row is removed and the leg shown in its place.
    - **Possible — check it's a journey**: found only by the word "to", such as "Continue to Angel Fire". Unticked; tick it only if it really is a journey.
3. Pick the city for any end left blank (for example München → Munich) so it's drawn on the map.
4. Click **Convert the ticked rows**.

The table becomes a travel section: converted rows and "Total" rows go, other rows stay for reference. No times are guessed; add them once booked.

## Clean-up suggestions

On the trip page, editors see **Tidy suggestions** in the Overview. The page lists, day by day, text that repeats something recorded elsewhere:

| Suggestion | For example | What applying it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A line repeats a journey, stay, meal or activity | "Check out Hotel Hessischer Hof after breakfast" | Removes the line; optionally adds its extra words to the item's notes. |
| A booking reminder repeats a task | "📅 Book: ICE seats for 6" | Deletes the reminder, or keeps its text as an info note on the task's item. |
| A phone number in the text | "Call ahead: 06033 65147" | Makes a contact, linked to the meal or plan, and removes the number from the text. |
| Trip notes that belong elsewhere | A hotels line, or a numbered list of options | Removes the line, or makes a decision with those options. |
| A table that describes an activity | A tour's times and meeting point | Moves the rows into an info note on the activity. |
| A "Day N" that doesn't match | "Day 3 is a travel day" on Day 4 | Nothing: marked **Check by hand**. |

Everything starts unticked. Tick what you agree with, choose an action where there are two, and click **Apply the ticked suggestions**. **Exact match** marks the safest ones. If the text changed since the page was opened, that suggestion is skipped and reported. **Not this** hides a suggestion for good.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A converted leg has the wrong city | Edit the leg and pick the right **From city** or **To city**. |
| A suggestion was applied by mistake | The removed text isn't kept. Add it back by editing the section, and **Not this** the suggestion if it returns. |
| A suggestion keeps coming back | Click **Not this**. |
