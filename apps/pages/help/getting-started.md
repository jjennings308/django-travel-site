# Getting started

ShareBucketList keeps the things you want to do someday and the trips you take to do them, in one place, shared with the people you travel with.

## From an idea to a trip

| Step | What you do | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Discover | Browse activities, events and places | **Discover** in the left menu |
| 2. Dream | Add the ones you want to your bucket list, or a goal of your own | **Bucket list** (step 1 under **Your journey**) |
| 3. Pick a date | Choose when, or the event date for an activity | **My dates** (step 2) |
| 4. Plan | Turn goals into a trip: days, journeys, stays, bookings | **Trips** (step 3) |
| 5. Done | Tick goals off, add photos, rate what you did | **Adventures** (step 4), and **Albums** for your photos |

The left menu (on a phone, ☰ at the top left) starts with **Home**, then **Discover**, then **Your journey**: the four steps in order, each with its stage underneath. **About** and **Help** are at the bottom.

When you take a goal to a trip (**Plan a trip** or **Add to this trip** on the goal), it lands on the trip's **Ideas** shelf, where everyone on the trip can see it and vote, and an editor puts it on a day ([ideas and goals](/help/ideas-and-goals/)). The quick way to lay out the trip's cities is **Set the route**: list the cities and how many nights in each, and the days, journeys between them and a stay in each fill themselves in ([starting a trip](/help/starting-a-trip/#the-route)).

## Home

**Home** is where you land after logging in. Under the greeting, **Add a goal** and **Plan a trip** start the two
things you'll do most. Three cards follow:

- **Next trip**: your next trip (or the one you're on), how many days to go, its cities and how many of its to-dos are
  done. **Open trip** goes to it.
- **Bucket list**: how many goals you've done out of all of them, and three you haven't, each with its stage (idea,
  dated, trip planned).
- **Needs you**: open to-dos on trips that haven't ended (the most pressing first), finished trips asking you to
  [rate their places](/help/places-and-events/#rating-a-finished-trip), and anything you added that was sent back to you for
  changes or not approved in the last three months.

Recommendations and your badges are underneath.

## Your first three things

Home shows these with a tick as you do them; **Hide this** puts them away.

1. **Add a goal.** On **My bucket list**, click **Add a goal** and type what you want to do, or click **Add to bucket list** on any activity, event or place. See [your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/).
2. **Start a trip.** On **Trips**, click **New trip**, or open a goal and click **Plan a trip** to start one with its dates and places filled in. See [starting a trip](/help/starting-a-trip/).
3. **Invite someone.** On the trip page, click **People**, type their email and pick what they may do. See [inviting people](/help/sharing-and-access/#inviting-people).

## Good to know

- Trips are private: only the people you invite can open them.
- Your [travel profile](/help/travel-profile/) (passport, seat preference, loyalty programs) is only for you and whoever books.
- You can [download your data or delete your account](/help/your-data/) at any time.
