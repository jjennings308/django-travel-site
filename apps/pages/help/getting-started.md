# Getting started

ShareBucketList keeps the things you want to do someday and the trips you take to do them, in one place, shared with the people you travel with.

## From an idea to a trip

| Step | What you do | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Discover | Browse activities, events and places | **Discover** in the left menu |
| 2. Dream | Add the ones you want to your bucket list, or a goal of your own | **My bucket list** |
| 3. Pick a date | Choose when, or the event date for an activity | **My dates** |
| 4. Plan | Turn goals into a trip: days, journeys, stays, bookings | **Trips** |
| 5. Done | Tick goals off, add photos, rate what you did | **My adventures** |

## Your first three things

Your dashboard shows these with a tick as you do them; **Hide this** puts them away.

1. **Add a goal.** On **My bucket list**, click **Add a goal** and type what you want to do, or click **Add to bucket list** on any activity, event or place. See [your bucket list](/help/bucket-list/).
2. **Start a trip.** On **Trips**, click **New trip**, or open a goal and click **Plan a trip** to start one with its dates and places filled in. See [starting a trip](/help/starting-a-trip/).
3. **Invite someone.** On the trip page, click **People**, type their email and pick what they may do. See [inviting people](/help/sharing-and-access/#inviting-people).

## Good to know

- Trips are private: only the people you invite can open them.
- Your [travel profile](/help/travel-profile/) (passport, seat preference, loyalty programs) is only for you and whoever books.
- You can [download your data or delete your account](/help/your-data/) at any time.
