# Sharing and access

A trip is private. Only people who have been given access to it can open it, and each person's access is set trip by trip.

## Who can do what

Each person on a trip is a **viewer**, a **commentor** or an **editor**. Each role can do everything the one before it can.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | Read the whole trip, its map, budget and photos, and download the PDF booklet. |
| Commentor | Also add notes on the trip, a day, a plan or a task ([comments and notes](/help/comments-and-notes/)), and add photos to the trip's album. |
| Editor | Also change everything: the trip's details, days, journeys, stays, activities, meals, confirmations, contacts and tasks, and share the trip. |

Deleting a trip needs editor access **and** being the person who created it.

The same account can be an editor on one trip and a viewer on another. If you open a trip you have no access to, the site says the page doesn't exist.

## Getting access

Access to a trip is given by the site's staff. To join a trip, or to be made an editor, ask the person who invited you, or [contact us](/contact/) with the trip's name.

To start trips of your own, you can use **Plan a trip** on a goal in your bucket list. You become an editor of the trip it creates.

## The public share link

An editor can make a read-only link that anyone can open, without an account. It shows a summary only:

| Shown | Never shown |
| --- | --- |
| The destination and the dates | The trip's own name, which often names who's going |
| How many nights, and the budget level (such as Moderate) | Who's travelling |
| Each day's number and weekday | The day's theme and write-up |
| How many plans each day has | Trains, flights and stays |
| The restaurants: name, meal, cuisine and price | Confirmation numbers, contacts, tasks and costs |

The shared page ends with "This is a summary. Travel details, bookings and confirmation numbers are not published."

## Sharing and stopping sharing

1. On the trip page, click **⋯** next to **Download PDF**.
2. Click **Share a read-only link**. The menu now shows the **Shared link**.
3. Click **Copy** and send the link to whoever you like.

To turn the link off, open **⋯** and click **Stop sharing**. The old link stops working at once. Sharing again makes a new link.

Anyone who can see the trip can open **⋯** to copy a shared link; only editors can share or stop sharing.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Page not found" when opening a trip | You don't have access to that trip. Ask the person who invited you. |
| No **⋯** button on the trip page | The trip isn't shared and you aren't an editor. |
| No **Share a read-only link** in the menu | Only editors can share. The trip may also already be shared: look for **Shared link**. |
| A shared link stopped working | Sharing was stopped, or stopped and started again. Ask an editor for the new link. |
