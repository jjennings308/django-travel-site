# Changes and undo

Every change to a trip's journeys, stays, activities, meals, days' plans and confirmations is recorded: what changed, who changed it and when. Click **History** at the top of the trip page (next to **Map**, **Budget**, **Photos** and **People**) to see the list, newest first. Anyone who can see the trip can read it.

## Reading the history

Each line says who made the change and what it was, for example "Ed: Changed Frankfurt Hbf → Augsburg Hbf: departs 10:00 AM CEST → 10:30 AM CEST". Click a line to go to that item on the trip page.

Use **Day**, **Person** and **Kind** (added, changed, removed) to narrow the list, then **Show**. **Imports and scripts** under **Person** finds changes made by importing an itinerary or by the site itself.

Large jobs, like importing an itinerary or applying tidy suggestions, show as one line, such as "Applied tidy suggestions (6 changes)".

The history keeps 180 days.

## Undo a change

You need [editor access](/help/sharing-and-access/#who-can-do-what).

1. Open **History**.
2. Find the change and click **Undo**.

The item goes back to how it was before that change. Undoing is itself a change, so it shows in the history too and can be undone in turn.

**Undo** only shows on an item's most recent change. If an item was changed several times, undo the newest first. If it was edited in a way the history didn't record, the site says so and changes nothing, so no edit is lost.

## Bring back something removed

If a journey, stay, activity, meal or one of a day's plans was removed by mistake, find its "Removed …" line in **History** and click **Restore**. It comes back as it was, with its confirmation numbers, linked contacts and notes, its travellers and any booking tasks that pointed at it.

Whole days and confirmations can't be restored: add them again. (A confirmation's tickets are deleted with it, so keep a copy before removing one.) A deleted trip can only be recovered by the site's staff; ask using the [contact page](/contact/).

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Undo** button | You're not an editor, it isn't the item's most recent change, or it was an addition (remove the item instead). |
| "Its day was removed, so there's nowhere to put it back" | The day it was on has gone. Add the day again first, then restore. |
| A change isn't in the history | Changes made before October 18, 2026 weren't recorded, and the history keeps 180 days. |
