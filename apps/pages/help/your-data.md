# Your data: download it or delete your account

Both are on one page: open the menu under your name, click **Settings**, then **Download my data** or **Delete my account** (or go straight to [Your data](/account/data/)).

## Download your data

Click **Download my data**. You get a zip file with:

| File | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| `account.json` | Your account, settings, profile and travel preferences |
| `travel_profile.json` | Your travel profile: passport and trusted-traveler details, and your loyalty programs |
| `bucket_list.json` | Your goals, their categories and your badges |
| `reviews.json`, `trip_notes.json` | Reviews you wrote, and notes you left on trips |
| `trips.json` | Every trip you're on, as you can see it (other people appear by name only) |
| `contributions.json` | Activities, events and places you added to the catalogue |
| `notifications.json` | Your notifications |
| `files/` | The photos and tickets you uploaded |

The file is made when you click and isn't kept on our server. You can download it again after 10 minutes.

## Delete your account

1. On [Your data](/account/data/), under **Delete your account**, type your password.
2. Tick the box to confirm and click **Delete my account**.

You're signed out, your account is hidden (your public bucket list and profile disappear), and we send you no more emails except one confirming the date. **After 30 days it's deleted for good. Signing in before then cancels it.**

When it's deleted:

| What | Happens |
| --- | --- |
| Your bucket list, photos, travel profile, loyalty programs, notifications, calendar links | Deleted |
| Trips you started that other people are on | Stay with them: the longest-standing other editor takes them over (or, if there's none, the person who's been on the trip longest becomes an editor) |
| Trips only you are on | Deleted, with their photos and tickets |
| Tickets you added to a shared trip | Stay with that trip's bookings |
| Your reviews and notes on shared trips | Stay, shown as from "A former member" |
| Places, events and activities you added to the catalogue | Stay, without your name |

Our backups keep copies for up to 12 months before they expire; we never use them to bring an account back.

## When our terms change

When we change our Terms of Service or Privacy policy, the next time you sign in or open a page you're asked to read and accept the new version before carrying on. The pages you need stay open while you decide: the [Terms](/terms/), [Privacy policy](/privacy/), Help, and this page if you'd rather delete your account. Each policy shows its version near the top.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That isn't your password" | Type the password you sign in with. Forgot it? Reset it from the sign-in page first. |
| "You downloaded your data a few minutes ago" | Wait 10 minutes and try again. |
| I asked to delete my account by mistake | Sign in within 30 days; it's cancelled and nothing is lost. |
